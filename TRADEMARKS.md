endorsed by, or sponsored by** Signal Messenger LLC or the Signal Foundation,
nor by any other company named in this repository's documentation.

cryptographic implementation is an independent, clean-room implementation
written from those published specifications. The independent-development
boundary that governs this, and the policy governing it, are set out in the
`tacenta-core` repository's `ADR-0003` and `ADR-0005`.
