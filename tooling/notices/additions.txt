tacenta-core (Apache-2.0)
-------------------------
This product embeds tacenta-core, Natuvea's independent, clean-room
implementation of the cryptographic protocol, licensed under Apache-2.0. Its
source and full licence are at https://github.com/Natuvea/tacenta-core.

Mozilla UniFFI runtime (MPL-2.0): native Android and Swift artifacts
--------------------------------------------------------------------
The Android (.aar) and Swift (xcframework) artifacts include runtime code
generated with and linked against Mozilla's UniFFI, which is licensed under
the Mozilla Public License 2.0. The full MPL-2.0 text appears in the licence
list above. As MPL-2.0 §3.2 requires, the corresponding source for the
MPL-covered files is available at https://github.com/mozilla/uniffi-rs at the
version pinned in this project's Cargo.lock; you may obtain it there at no
charge.

JNA, Java Native Access (Apache-2.0): Android artifact only
------------------------------------------------------------
The Android (.aar) artifact declares a dependency on JNA (net.java.dev.jna),
used under its Apache-2.0 licence. (JNA is dual-licensed Apache-2.0 or
LGPL-2.1; the Apache-2.0 terms are selected here.) The full Apache-2.0 text
appears in the licence list above.
